## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one cultural badge cut from paper, not a narrative and not a digital logo. Use a bold outer contour, a few overlapping shapes and positive-negative relation. Allow the subject to be incomplete, exaggerated or symbolic. Material is paper-cut, stencil, screenprint and crayon grain, with misregistration. Color is exactly one main, one support and one accent, taken from the photograph: soft blue, plant green, warm yellow, coral, ivory or ochre. The badge is small and may rotate slightly. Type is a tiny cultural note. Reject a full illustration, smooth vector, AI cartoon, 3D and a template icon.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-308/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
